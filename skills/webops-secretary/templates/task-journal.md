## Action ledger

Record intent before mutation, then update after independent verification. Reuse the same operation key on reconciliation; do not create a new key to get around an uncertain result.

| Operation key | Action and opaque target | Preconditions checked | State | Verified result or uncertainty | Updated at |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Action states: `planned`, `in_flight`, `verified`, `failed`, `unknown`. `failed` requires evidence that the intended effect did not occur; an ambiguous network timeout is `unknown`. Both `in_flight` and `unknown` require reconciliation before dependent work.
